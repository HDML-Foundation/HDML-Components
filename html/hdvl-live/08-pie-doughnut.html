08 — Pie and doughnut: two measures that disagree

Four figures over five slices, and the reason to look at them with real numbers is that the two measures do not agree. Apple is 58.7 % of every MAANG share that changed hands and only 38.4 % of every MAANG dollar; Microsoft is 7.4 % of the shares and 19.9 % of the dollars.

hdml-pie is a layout widget: it derives cumulative fractions internally, so its angle domain is always 0…1, authored and never derived. Because row order is slice order, its source frame must pin a sort (V7) — an unsorted query may legally reorder between refreshes and the pie would visibly rearrange.

Full share table, alphabetical (which is also the slice order): Amazon 17.71 % / 21.64 %, Apple 58.74 % / 38.43 %, Google 14.03 % / 12.58 %, Microsoft 7.44 % / 19.93 %, Netflix 2.09 % / 7.41 % — shares first, dollars second.
