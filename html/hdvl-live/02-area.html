02 — Area: a numeric baseline and a categorical one

Two forms of the same element. A is the ordinary numeric area: y0="0" is a scalar broadcast to every row. B is the categorical form — y0 is a string constant, y1 is a column of category names, and both land on the same band scale, so the kinds match (V2).
