04 — Grouped and stacked: one rename apart

A, B, C and D have byte-identical children. Only the container tag and the y domain change. That is the claim the page exists to test: grouped ↔ stacked is not a mode flag, a config object or a different chart type — it is hdml-cluster vs hdml-stack.

Annual share volume for Amazon, Apple and Microsoft over the ten complete years 2013–2022. Google and Netflix are left out so that three series meet a three-entry palette exactly — the case where a palette-exhaustion bug cannot hide.

Two fields exist purely to feed scales and are bound by no widget: series_max (the tallest individual bar) and total (the tallest stack). A stack never feeds a scale, and there is no widget-union fallback.
