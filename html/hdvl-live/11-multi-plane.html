11 — Multiple planes: panels and an overlay

A plane contributes no dimension of its own — it is the geometric anchor that the scale chains build their spaces in. Scales never cross a plane boundary, because a range belongs to its plane's geometry. Data and domains do cross: source is inherited, identical refs coalesce into one query, and a shared values= is what makes panels comparable.

B is not a dual axis. Page 07 puts two coordinate systems on one plot region as sibling scales; this puts two plot regions — each with its own padding, guides and chain — on one canvas.
