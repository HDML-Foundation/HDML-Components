05 — Scatter: one cloud, two y scales

One frame, 114 points — one per (stock, year) over 2000–2023, five stocks and up to 24 years each; Google starts in 2004 and Netflix in 2002, which is why it is 114 and not 120.

B is the figure that needs the log, and not for decoration: mean close per stock-year spans four orders of magnitude, because the prices are split-adjusted at source and the early Apple and Amazon years sit near zero. Note that B carries no min="0" — a log domain that touches zero is a V2 error and projects nothing.
