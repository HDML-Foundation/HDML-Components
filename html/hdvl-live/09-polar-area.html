09 — Polar area: value to radius, not to angle

Page 08 binds the measure to angle and gets a pie, which needs a layout widget. This page binds it to radius and gets a polar area, which needs no layout exception at all: equal angular slices fall out of the band scale on the angle channel, and the mark is an ordinary hdml-arc.

Both radius scales are type="sqrt", so area is proportional to value. That is what makes the wedge lengths look less dramatic than the numbers — and it is correct.
