The smallest legal live render. Two scales, four guides, one mark, one frame — and no stylesheet rule that names an hdml-* element. The figure below is styled by nothing but width: 480px; everything else you see is a spec default: the view's 2:1 aspect ratio, the UA plane padding the guides land in, x guides under the plot and y guides left of it, bandwidth 0.8, currentColor marks and 11px system-ui labels.

The whole page

Renders. Mean daily share volume for each of the five MAANG stocks, 2000-01-03 → 2023-03-17.

Grammar. hdml-cartesian-plane → hdml-ordinal-scale channel="x" (domain subscribed to the label column, so row order is the domain) → hdml-continuous-scale channel="y" (domain authored 0…450000000) → hdml-axis ×2, hdml-label ×2, one hdml-bar. Frame sym_volume: 5 rows, avg(p_v) grouped by stock, sorted descending.

Validate.

  • Five bars, in this order left to right: Apple, Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Netflix — the sort is on the measure, and row order is what the ordinal domain is.
  • Heights, against the 450 M ceiling: Apple 411.3 M (≈91 % of full height), Amazon 124.0 M, Google 122.6 M, Microsoft 52.1 M, Netflix 16.3 M.
  • Amazon and Google are 1.1 % apart — the two bars should be all but indistinguishable. If they are visibly different, that is a bug.
  • The y axis carries 5 labels in compact notation (0, 100M … 450M or the tick algorithm's nearest equivalent) and the x axis carries the five names, unrotated.
  • The figure is 480px wide and half that tall. Nothing is clipped; the leftmost y label is inside the view.
